# 05 — Go-To-Market Playbook

**Objective: 24 paying clients in 12 months, from a standing start, in one city, with ₹4.45 lakh.**

The constraint is not capital or technology. It is **audits booked per week**. Everything below
exists to keep that number above eight.

---

## 1. The funnel

```
  Conversations        160/month  ──┐
        ↓ 20%                       │  Field + referral + partner + content
  Leak Audits booked    32/month  ──┘
        ↓ 40%
  Recovery Sprints       13/month  (capped at 3 concurrent → realistically 3–4/month)
        ↓ 50%
  Paid clients          ~2/month early, 4/month by M9
```

Because pilots are capped at three concurrent, **the real throttle is delivery, not demand.**
Once the build time falls from 40 hours to 12, the cap can lift to six.

---

## 2. Five channels, in priority order

### Channel 1 — Field walk-ins (the engine, months 1–6)

Hyderabad's healthcare is geographically clustered. That is a gift: 20 qualified doorsteps in a
single afternoon, on foot.

| Cluster | What's there | Best time |
|---|---|---|
| **Somajiguda / Ameerpet / Punjagutta** | Highest density of diagnostic centres and specialist clinics | Tue–Thu, 11 am–1 pm |
| **Banjara Hills / Jubilee Hills** | Dental, derm, IVF, cosmetic, premium polyclinics | Tue–Thu, 3–5 pm |
| **Secunderabad / Paradise / Tarnaka** | Established labs, older practices, high volume | Wed–Fri, 11 am–1 pm |
| **KPHB / Kukatpally / Miyapur** | Dense residential; family clinics, mid-size labs | Sat, 11 am–2 pm |
| **Dilsukhnagar / LB Nagar / Malakpet** | High-volume, price-sensitive, very low digital adoption | Tue–Thu, 4–6 pm |
| **Gachibowli / Kondapur / Madhapur** | Corporate-adjacent, newer clinics, highest willingness to pay | Mon–Fri, 3–5 pm |
| **Attapur / Mehdipatnam / Tolichowki** | Underserved by every agency; Urdu-language advantage | Tue–Thu, 11 am–1 pm |

**The walk-in script (say this to the receptionist, not the doctor):**

> *"Hi — I work with diagnostic centres around Somajiguda on reducing missed appointments. I'm not
> selling software. I do a free 45-minute check that tells the owner exactly how much money empty
> slots cost them each month. Is [owner name] in this week, or should I leave the one-pager?"*

**Rules:**
- Never ask to "meet the doctor". Ask for a **time**.
- Leave a one-page Leak Report *sample* (a filled-in anonymised example), not a brochure.
- Log everything the same evening. 20 doors × 4 days = 80 conversations/week → target 8 audits.
- Expect 3–5 no's for every maybe. This is a numbers exercise, not a persuasion exercise.

### Channel 2 — Referral loop (becomes the engine from month 5)

Owner-doctors are the densest referral network in Indian business — every lab owner knows 30
referring doctors and every doctor knows five labs.

- **₹10,000 credit to the referrer** for every referral that converts to a paid client
- Ask at exactly two moments: (a) the day the first ROI report shows a positive number,
  (b) month 3, after the second good report. Never at signature.
- The ask, verbatim: *"Who are the two people you'd want to have this before your competitors do?"*
  — two names, not "anyone you know".
- Offer to run the free Leak Audit **as a favour in their name**. That framing gets the WhatsApp
  introduction on the spot.

**Target: 40% of clients from referral by month 9.** If it is under 25% by month 6, your delivery is
not good enough to talk about — fix delivery, not marketing.

### Channel 3 — Partner channel (highest leverage, slowest to start)

| Partner type | Why they help | The deal |
|---|---|---|
| **LIS / HIS / clinic-software vendors** | They have installed bases and no automation layer; automation makes their software stickier | 15% of first-year revenue, or reciprocal referral |
| **Medical equipment & consumables dealers** | They visit every lab in the city monthly, already trusted | ₹10,000 per converted introduction |
| **CAs and consultants serving clinics** | They see the P&L and the compliance exposure | Co-branded DPDP-readiness offering |
| **Interior/setup contractors for new clinics** | Every new clinic is a greenfield sale | Bundle into their fit-out package |
| **BSPs (AiSensy, Gallabox, Interakt)** | They need implementation partners for accounts they cannot service | Reseller margin + inbound leads |
| **Insurance TPAs** | Claim-status workflows | Volume introduction |

Start with **two** LIS/HIS vendors and **one** equipment dealer. One good dealer relationship is
worth more than a hundred cold walk-ins.

### Channel 4 — Associations and institutions (credibility, months 3+)

- **[FTCCI](https://www.ftcci.in/)** — join, then offer a free session to the health/MSME
  sub-committee: *"What the DPDP deadline of May 2027 means for your patient data"*. Sell nothing.
- **IMA Telangana branches, AHPI** — CME-adjacent slots and branch WhatsApp groups
- **[MSME-DFO Hyderabad](https://msmedihyderabad.gov.in/)** — training programmes and scheme
  awareness sessions; only 2.7% of MSMEs know these schemes exist, so this is genuinely useful
- **T-Hub / TASK / WE Hub / RICH** — credibility, hiring pipeline, and a possible incubation route
- **BDMAI, HGTA, CREDAI Hyderabad** — pre-build relationships now for verticals 2 and 3

**The talk that opens every door:** *"₹250 crore: what the DPDP Rules mean for a Hyderabad clinic."*
Compliance fear opens rooms that sales pitches cannot.

### Channel 5 — Content (compounding, low cost)

- **LinkedIn, 3×/week** — the "Leak Math" series. One anonymised clinic, one number, one chart.
  *"This lab lost ₹1.4 lakh last month to a reminder that went out at 9 pm."*
- **Instagram/YouTube Shorts in Telugu, 2×/week** — 45 seconds, front-desk pain, no jargon. Telugu
  business content in this niche is essentially uncontested.
- **One long-form piece per month** — *"The DPDP Compliance Checklist for Hyderabad Clinics"*,
  *"What Meta's January 2026 policy change means for healthcare WhatsApp bots"*. These rank, and
  they are true — almost nobody else has read the source documents.
- **A public Leak Calculator on the website** — enter appointments/month, no-show %, average value;
  get a rupee figure and a "book a free audit" button. This is your best inbound asset.

Content budget: **₹10,000/month max** in year 1. This channel is for compounding trust, not for
volume. Volume comes from your feet.

## 3. Target list construction (week 1)

Build a 400-row spreadsheet before you knock on a single door:

| Source | What to extract |
|---|---|
| Google Maps, by cluster | Name, area, phone, rating, **review count** (a proxy for patient volume) |
| JustDial / Practo / Sulekha listings | Type, specialities, branch count |
| Telangana Clinical Establishments / health-department listings | Registration status |
| Lab-chain franchise directories | Franchisees are ideal — SME budget, corporate-grade expectations |
| LinkedIn | Owner name (the difference between a pitch and a conversation) |

**Priority scoring:**

| Signal | Score |
|---|---|
| 100+ Google reviews (real volume) | +3 |
| 2–5 branches (complex enough to need us, small enough to decide fast) | +3 |
| Rating below 4.2 (they feel the pain) | +2 |
| Website has online booking (already digitally willing) | +2 |
| Listed in a corporate/insurance panel (steady volume) | +2 |
| Single doctor, no staff | −3 |
| Part of a national chain (procurement committee) | −4 |

Work the top 120 first. Refresh monthly.

## 4. Weekly operating rhythm (founder, months 1–6)

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Delivery + pipeline review + weekly ROI reports to clients | Build/config |
| **Tue** | Field: 20 doors | 2 Leak Audits |
| **Wed** | Field: 20 doors | 2 Leak Audits |
| **Thu** | Field: 20 doors | 2 Leak Audits + partner meeting |
| **Fri** | Build/config | Results reviews & closes |
| **Sat** | Field (KPHB/Dilsukhnagar — Saturday is a working day for clinics) | Content + templatisation |
| **Sun** | Off. Non-negotiable. | |

**Non-negotiables:** 60 doors/week, 6 audits/week, one templatisation improvement/week (build hours
must fall every single week), one ROI report per client per month — sent before they ask.

## 5. Hiring plan

| Month | Role | Cost/month | Trigger to hire |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Automation engineer** | ₹45,000 | 7 live clients, founder build time > 60% |
| 8 | **Field sales associate** (Telugu-first) | ₹25,000 + ₹5,000/close | Audit bookings < 6/week for 3 straight weeks |
| 9 | **Client success / ops associate** | ₹28,000 | 12 live clients |
| 14 | 2nd engineer + 2nd sales associate | ₹80,000 combined | 35 live clients |
| 18 | Vertical lead — distribution | ₹70,000 | Vertical 2 has 10 clients |

Recruit from **TASK**, from local BCA/MCA colleges, and from front-desk staff at clinics you have
already automated — the last group is the best hire in the business: they know the workflow, the
language and the customer, and they cost less than an engineer to train on n8n.

## 6. Sales assets to build in week 1

1. **The one-page Leak Report template** (see [03](03-service-catalogue-and-pricing.md))
2. **The anonymised sample Leak Report** — the only leave-behind you carry
3. **The 2-page Pilot Agreement** with the commitment clause
4. **The 2-page results one-pager** template
5. **MSA + SOW + DPA + NDA** — get these drafted properly by a lawyer, once (~₹25,000). This is the
   best money you will spend all year.
6. **A 6-slide deck** — used only when a prospect insists. Slides: the leak, the baseline, the four
   workflows, the compliance architecture, the pricing, the guarantee.
7. **The Leak Calculator web page**
8. **Three WhatsApp follow-up sequences** — post-audit, post-pilot, dormant-revival
